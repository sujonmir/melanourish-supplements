      Why supplements made for Black women?

      Some nutrient gaps are more common in Black women, for example vitamin D, because melanin reduces how much the skin makes from sunlight. Our formulas focus on those nutrients.

      Our supplements are made and shipped from GMP-certified facilities in the United States.
